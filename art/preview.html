M5TVset toy TV

A preview of all 11 channels of a toy TV for a brick-built house. These are the same PNG frames the firmware shows, with the same timings.

Now on air 1 · News frame 1 of 8
    • KEY1 (→ or Enter): 0.4 s of static, then the next channel. Its number stays in the corner for 2 s. After the last channel comes the first.
    • KEY2 (Space): the screen shuts off like an old CRT. Any key turns it back on at the same channel.
    • 3 minutes without a key press: the TV turns itself off.
    • The channel chips are only for this preview. On the board, KEY1 cycles through the channels.

    Frame numbers match the files in art/frames/<NN>_<channel>/<MM>.png. NN is the channel number and MM is the frame number minus one, both zero-padded. For example, Sports frame 4 is art/frames/05_sport/03.png.
